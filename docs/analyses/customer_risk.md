# Customer Repeat & Risk Intelligence

![Tableau Customer Repeat & Risk Dashboard](../assets/5_customer_risk.png)

The Customer Repeat & Risk Intelligence dashboard connects customer retention analytics with fraud-risk monitoring. It shows where Nova depends on repeat behavior, where suspicious activity appears, and which markets or categories need closer review.

!!! abstract "What this dashboard answers"

    - Which categories rely most on repeat customers?
    - How much revenue comes from repeat behavior?
    - Where are suspicious transactions concentrated?
    - Which markets should be prioritized for risk monitoring?

## Fraud Detection Methodology

The project uses an unsupervised anomaly detection approach with an Isolation Forest model. Since the dataset does not include labeled fraud outcomes, the model identifies transactions that look unusual compared with normal user and transaction behavior.

??? info "Features used by the model"

    - Transaction amount and transaction hour
    - User transaction frequency over 1-hour and 24-hour windows
    - User spending in the previous 24 hours
    - Difference from historical user spending behavior
    - Cross-market and cross-service activity
    - Late-night activity
    - Failed and refunded transaction signals

The model produces a fraud anomaly score. Higher scores indicate more unusual transaction behavior and higher review priority.

## KPI Cards

| KPI | What it shows | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Repeat Revenue Share | Share of revenue generated by repeat customers | Measures revenue sustainability |
| Revenue per Repeat User | Average revenue from each repeat customer | Indicates repeat-customer value |
| Repeat Interaction Rate | Share of interactions that come from repeat behavior | Tracks engagement depth |
| Suspicious Amount | Total value of suspicious transactions | Quantifies financial exposure |
| Suspicious Rate | Share of transactions flagged as suspicious | Tracks fraud-risk level |
| Highest Risk Market | Market with the highest fraud concentration | Guides targeted monitoring |
| Fraud Score | Model-generated anomaly score | Provides a consolidated risk indicator |

## Dashboard Views

### Repeat Revenue Dependency by Category

This view shows which categories depend most heavily on repeat customers.

!!! tip "How to read it"

    High repeat revenue dependency can be a strength when customers are loyal, but it also creates retention risk if those customers churn.

### Fraud Risk by Category

This ranking compares categories by suspicious transaction volume and suspicious amount.

Use it to identify where fraud controls, manual review, or monitoring rules should be tightened first.

### Fraud Activity Trend

The trend view tracks suspicious activity over time.

| Business question | Dashboard signal |
| --- | --- |
| Are fraud risks increasing? | Rising suspicious transaction count or suspicious amount |
| Are there abnormal spikes? | Sudden changes in fraud score or flagged volume |
| Is risk seasonal or event-driven? | Concentrated spikes during specific periods |

### High-Risk Markets Overview

The market view breaks down suspicious transaction count, suspicious rate, and suspicious amount by geography.

!!! warning "Business use"

    A high-risk market is not automatically a bad market. It means the market deserves closer investigation, tighter monitoring, or more targeted fraud controls.

## Business Impact

This dashboard helps stakeholders improve retention strategy, quantify repeat-customer value, detect anomalous transaction patterns, monitor fraud exposure, and prioritize risk investigations across Nova's markets and categories.
